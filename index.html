Why Video Upscaling Is Not Just Image Upscaling, Repeated

An image upscaler can be wrong per-pixel and nobody notices. A video upscaler that is wrong per-pixel produces visible flicker, crawling edges, and texture that boils between frames. Temporal consistency is the hard part, not resolution.
